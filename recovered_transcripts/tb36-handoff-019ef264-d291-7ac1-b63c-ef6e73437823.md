current runtime validation rejects mixed-family MXFP4 plane-split storage.

Primary blocker:

```text
ssd/ssd_flash_moe_sidecar.c
```

Around the loader validation, this check rejects mixed layout:

```text
family_mxfp4_plane_split[gate] != family_mxfp4_plane_split[up] ||
family_mxfp4_plane_split[gate] != family_mxfp4_plane_split[down]
```

That guard must be replaced with a supported mixed-layout policy. For this
experiment, the desired supported combination is:

