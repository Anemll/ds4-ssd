This is an excellent question that gets to the heart of how modern key-value (KV) stores (like RocksDB, LevelDB, or WiredTiger) manage data to balance performance and storage efficiency.

The core difference is that **raw KV rows** prioritize simplicity and speed of access, while **compressed KV rows** prioritize storage efficiency and reduced I/O bandwidth, at the cost of additional CPU overhead.

Here is a detailed breakdown of the differences.

### Raw KV Rows

A raw KV row stores the key and value in their original, uncompressed form, exactly as provided by the application.
