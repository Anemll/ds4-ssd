## Why Edge Cases Matter for CLI Parsers

Command-line parsers are the gatekeepers of user input. Edge cases often reveal:
- **Security vulnerabilities** (e.g., injection attacks via flags)
- **Usability bugs** (e.g., confusing error messages)
- **Logic errors** (e.g., incorrect parsing of combined short flags)
- **Cross-platform issues** (e.g., path separators, encoding)
