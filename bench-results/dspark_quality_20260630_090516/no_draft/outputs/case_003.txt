This is an excellent question because it gets at the fundamental design choice behind RMSNorm (Root Mean Square Normalization) versus the more traditional LayerNorm (Layer Normalization).

The core reason RMSNorm needs both a **sum of squares** (to compute the Root Mean Square) and a **scaling pass** (to divide by it) is that it is designed to be a **purely statistical normalization** that removes the scale of the input vector, while preserving its direction and structure.
