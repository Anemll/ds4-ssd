1.  **CPU Utilization (Average & Peak)**
    - **Why it's useful:** This tells you how hard your server's processor is working. Consistently high CPU usage (e.g., >80%) indicates your server is under-provisioned or has a performance bottleneck (like a tight loop or inefficient algorithm). It helps you plan capacity and identify abnormal spikes from issues like DDoS attacks or runaway processes.
