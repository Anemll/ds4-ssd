This is an excellent question that gets at the heart of how we manage concurrent access to data.

The core difference is one of **purpose and mechanism**: a mutex is a **locking mechanism** for protecting complex data structures, while an atomic counter is a **hardware-level instruction** for performing simple integer operations without locks.
