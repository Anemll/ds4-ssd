This is a great question that gets to the heart of how LLMs actually run on hardware.

The core reason is that **memory bandwidth is a much bigger bottleneck than compute** for single-token decode, while **compute is the bottleneck** for prefill.

Here’s the breakdown of why a prefill (processing a large prompt) is faster *per token* (or in terms of throughput) than a single-token decode.
