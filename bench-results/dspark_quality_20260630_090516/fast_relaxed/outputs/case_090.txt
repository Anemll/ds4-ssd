## Role of Calibration Data in Neural Network Quantization

Calibration data plays a critical role in the quantization process, particularly for **post-training quantization (PTQ)** methods. Here's a detailed breakdown:

### Core Purpose

Calibration data is used to **determine the optimal range (min/max values)** for weights and activations before converting them from floating-point (FP32) to lower-precision formats (INT8, INT4, etc.). This ensures minimal accuracy loss during quantization.
