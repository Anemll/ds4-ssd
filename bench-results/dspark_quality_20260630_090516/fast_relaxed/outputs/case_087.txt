Here are three essential server metrics, along with explanations of why they are useful for monitoring and maintaining a healthy system:

### 1. CPU Utilization (Percentage)
**What it is:** The percentage of time the CPU is busy processing instructions (as opposed to being idle).

**Why it's useful:**
- **Capacity Planning:** If CPU consistently exceeds 80-90%, it indicates the server is under-provisioned for its workload. This helps you decide whether to scale up (more CPU cores) or scale out (more servers).
