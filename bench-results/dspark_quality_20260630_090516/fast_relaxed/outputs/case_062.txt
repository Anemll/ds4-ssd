## Eventual Consistency

**Eventual consistency** is a consistency model in distributed databases where, after a write, all nodes will eventually see the updated data, but not immediately. It's a "weak" consistency guarantee compared to **strong consistency**.

### Key Characteristics

1. **No immediate guarantee**: After a write, reads may return stale data for a period
2. **Eventually**: All replicas will converge to the same state, given no new writes
3. **Temporal delay**: The "eventual" period can be milliseconds or longer
