This is a great question that gets to a core tension in **concurrent systems** and **distributed databases**.

The short answer is: **A single large buffer creates a single point of contention and a bottleneck**, while multiple overlapping views (like in a multi-version or sharded system) allow for **parallelism** and **reduced contention**.
