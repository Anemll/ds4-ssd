This is a great question that gets at the heart of how large-scale Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models are optimized for inference.

The core idea of **Grouped Expert Execution** is a **hardware-aware optimization**. It doesn't change the mathematical output of the MoE layer, but it changes the *order* and *packing* of the computation to maximize throughput and minimize latency, especially during the **prefill** phase.
