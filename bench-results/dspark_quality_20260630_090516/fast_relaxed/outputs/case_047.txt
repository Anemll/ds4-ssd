## Raw KV Rows vs. Compressed KV Rows

**Raw KV Rows** and **Compressed KV Rows** are two different storage formats for key-value data, primarily used in databases and storage systems. Here's a detailed comparison:

### Raw KV Rows

**Characteristics:**
- **Uncompressed format** - Data is stored as-is without any compression
- **Full key-value pairs** - Each row stores the complete key and value
- **Direct access** - No decompression overhead when reading
- **Higher storage footprint** - Takes more disk space
