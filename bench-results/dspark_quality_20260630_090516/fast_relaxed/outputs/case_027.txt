This is an excellent question that gets to the heart of modern quantization techniques.

The term "importance matrix" is not a standard, universally defined term in the same way as "weight matrix" or "activation matrix." However, in the context of **low-bit quantization**, it refers to a **matrix that captures the relative importance of different weights or activations for the final model's output.**
