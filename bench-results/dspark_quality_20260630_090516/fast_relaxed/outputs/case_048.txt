Here is a comprehensive checklist for validating that a quantized model still follows instructions effectively.

This checklist is designed to be model-agnostic and focuses on **behavioral consistency** rather than just numerical accuracy.

### Phase 1: Pre-Validation (Sanity & Baseline)

- [ ] **Define "Ground Truth":** Before quantizing, run the original model on a set of 10-20 diverse, complex instructions. Save the exact outputs (text, code, JSON, etc.) as the "gold standard" baseline.
