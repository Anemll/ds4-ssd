This is a great question because these two concepts are often confused, yet they are fundamentally different.

Think of it as a **water pipeline** going to your house.

- **Latency** is the **time** it takes for a single drop of water to travel from the reservoir to the tap.
- **Throughput** is the **rate** of flow, or the total volume of water that can come out of the tap per minute.
