This is a great question because these two terms are often confused, but they measure very different things. The best way to understand them is with an analogy.

### The Highway Analogy

Think of a system (like a computer, a network, or a database) as a highway connecting two cities.

- **Latency** is the **time** it takes for a single car to drive from point A to point B. It's about **speed**.
- **Throughput** is the **number of cars** that can arrive at point B per hour. It's about **capacity**.
