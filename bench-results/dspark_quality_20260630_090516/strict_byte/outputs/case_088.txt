**Great question!** Treating special tags carefully is crucial because tokenizers are the bridge between raw text and the numerical representations that models (like LLMs) actually process. If a tokenizer mishandles special tags, it can break the model's ability to understand structure, instructions, or formatting.

Here’s why special tags need careful treatment, broken down into key reasons:

### 1. **Preserving Structural Meaning (e.g., `<s>`, `</s>`, `[CLS]`)**
   - **Role:** These tags mark the start/end of sequences, separate sentences, or indicate special roles
