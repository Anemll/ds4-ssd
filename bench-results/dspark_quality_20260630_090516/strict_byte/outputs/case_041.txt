This is a great question that gets to the heart of how language models make decisions.

The short answer is: **Because the model chooses the *highest* logit, not the *most different* one. A tiny difference at the top can completely change the winner.**

Let's break down why small logit differences have such a big impact.

### 1. The "Greedy" Selection Mechanism

When a model generates text "greedily," it doesn't look at the whole landscape of possibilities. It simply picks the token with the **highest logit** (or highest probability after softmax).
