DSpark eval-margin + rate combined gate (2026-07-09)
====================================================

Env vars (all default-off / opt-in):
  DS4_DSPARK_EVAL_MARGIN_GATE_THRESHOLD=<float>
    Simple pre-draft margin-only gate (existing). Skips draft+verify when
    target eval margin < threshold. Best safe gate on this prompt: 4.5.

  DS4_DSPARK_EVAL_MARGIN_RATE_GATE=1
    Combined two-stage gate (NEW, default off):
      1) pre-draft: same margin threshold check
      2) post-draft: conf-truncated (default conf=0.4) then rate economics
         with allow_skip (k=0) using:
           eval_seconds_ema, draft_seconds_ema, dynamic_cost_ema[k],
           ds4_dspark_conf_calib_lookup()
    Alias: DS4_DSPARK_MARGIN_RATE_GATE=1

Stats:
  skip-pre    = pre-draft skips (margin / dormant)
  skip-verify = drafted but verify skipped (k=0)
  debug-status: skip:N vskip:M

Byte safety:
  cmp=0 for ungated vs margin vs combined at n=300/500/1000 (temp 0).

Measured (arcade HTML prompt, resident IQ2XXS, -c 20000, single process):

n=1000 (post-draft force k>=1 experiment — REJECTED):
  margin=4.5  39.52 t/s  tau=3.25  avg_sched=4.58
  combined    31.84 t/s  tau=0.97  avg_sched=1.17   (rate collapsed to k=1)

Conclusion (negative for combined gate vs margin-only):
  - Pre-draft margin threshold=4.5 remains the best SAFE opt-in gate.
  - Post-draft rate k=0 after a margin pass often wastes the already-paid
    draft (~10ms) on high-margin blocks (38 verify-skips @ n1000) and
    drops tau (3.25 -> 2.81). Net t/s is near-neutral / within noise,
    not the +0.5..+1.5 target over margin-only.
  - Forcing k>=1 after draft makes rate pick k=1 almost always (avg 1.17)
    and is a large regression.
  - Root cause: once draft is sunk, rate economics that can decline verify
    do not recover draft cost; conf-head calibration + cost EMAs are too
    aggressive at skipping high-margin opportunities that margin-only
    would verify profitably.
  - Keep combined gate default-OFF for further experiments; recommend
    DS4_DSPARK_EVAL_MARGIN_GATE_THRESHOLD=4.5 for production trials.
  - User should re-run -n 4000 locally for a less noisy long-ctx read.
