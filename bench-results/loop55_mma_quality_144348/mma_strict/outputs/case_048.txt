Here is a comprehensive checklist for validating that a quantized model still follows instructions. This checklist is designed to be model-agnostic and focuses on functional correctness rather than just numerical similarity.

### Phase 1: Sanity Checks (Pre-Validation)

**Objective:** Ensure the quantization process itself did not break the model's basic functionality.

- [ ] **1. Load & Inference Check:** Can the quantized model be loaded without errors? Does a single forward pass (inference) complete without crashing?
