### 1. **CPU Utilization (Percentage)**
- **What it measures:** The percentage of time the CPU spends processing instructions (as opposed to being idle).
- **Why it’s useful:** High CPU utilization (e.g., consistently above 80-90%) indicates the server is under heavy load, which can lead to slow response times or timeouts. Conversely, very low utilization might mean resources are being wasted.
