A **ring buffer** (also called a circular buffer) is a data structure that uses a fixed-size array as if it were connected end-to-end, forming a circle.

---

## How a Ring Buffer "Wraps"

### The core idea
You have:
- A fixed-size array (e.g., size `N`)
- Two pointers: **read** (head) and **write** (tail)
- Both pointers move forward, but when they reach the end of the array, they **wrap around** to index 0.
