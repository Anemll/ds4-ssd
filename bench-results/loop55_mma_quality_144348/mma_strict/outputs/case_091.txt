A confidence interval is a range of numbers that likely contains the true value you're trying to measure.

Think of it like this: If you're trying to guess the exact number of jelly beans in a giant jar, you probably won't get it right on the first try. Instead, you might say, "I'm pretty sure there are between 1,000 and 1,200 jelly beans."
